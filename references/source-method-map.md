## Methods to preserve

- Market breadth and index/sector leadership before individual-stock timing.
- Actual results versus consensus, guidance and the expectations already embedded in the price.
- Transparent forward-year valuation ranges with explicit earnings/multiple assumptions.
- Macro-to-equity causal chains and distinct monetary, fiscal and private-credit mechanisms.
- Positioning, leverage, systematic flows and options mechanics as conditional context.
- Support/resistance zones, volume confirmation, relative strength, and separate short/long horizons.
- Conditional action plans, deliberate entry/exit criteria, and updates when evidence changes.

## Claims not to make permanent rules

Do not universalize a historical sector dependency, typical seasonal path, valuation multiple, personal allocation, price floor, rebound probability, or options strategy. Do not infer institutional intent from volume, guarantee mean reversion, or claim a market must rise because its earnings forecast requires it. Refresh data and test the assumptions.
