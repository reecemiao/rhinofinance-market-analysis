# Output modes

Use the user's language and requested depth. Keep the reasoning conversational, precise and evidence-led; omit sponsor messages, membership pitches, catchphrases and claims to be the original presenter. Use original wording and avoid copying long passages.

## Full market review

1. Lead with market judgment, horizon and what changed.
2. Give a compact market table: index/sector, session move, breadth or relative strength, relevant level/condition and interpretation. Include only verified values.
3. Explain two to four decisive macro, earnings or flow drivers with expectation gaps and causal channels. Include counterevidence.
4. Discuss leading/lagging sectors and why a move may be broad, concentrated, rotational or mechanical.
5. Give cards for requested or genuinely decision-relevant stocks; do not invent a portfolio or cover every mentioned ticker.
6. End with conditional actions and a small catalyst/trigger watchlist with confirmed versus tentative dates and missing evidence.

## Stock/sector decision card

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| View now | Buy/add/hold/wait/trim/exit or evidence-limited; horizon; short reason |
| Evidence | Dated price, fundamentals and decisive recent change; direct citations |
| Valuation | Metric/period, assumptions and range; current implied expectation |
| Scenarios | Bear/base/bull outcomes and returns if useful; subjective weights only if warranted |
| Timing | Trend, relative volume/strength, entry/confirmation zone and next objective |
| Risk | Business thesis break, valuation risk and distinct tactical invalidation |
| Action alternatives | Why preferred action beats adding/holding/trimming now; what reverses it |
| Next check | Dated catalyst or objective evidence/price trigger; unresolved inputs |

Do not force an exact buy price when data do not support one. A holding decision must earn its place through prospective return, transaction friction or catalyst timing; a wait decision needs a condition and review point. Avoid a blanket hold bias or a forced buy/sell bias.

## Earnings/catalyst review

Lead with whether the thesis changed. Show expectations versus results/guidance; business KPIs; price/volume reaction versus market and sector; valuation revision; alternative explanations; action and next evidence. Separate immediate reaction from +5/+20-day behavior if those periods have elapsed and data exist.

## Update mode

Use a short delta table: prior dated view/trigger; new fact; implication; changed action; next review. Preserve the old view and record failed/expired triggers honestly. Do not append to a persistent tracker unless the task calls for it.

## Source-corpus or historical request

When asked what the presenter said, attribute the thesis to the file's spoken session date and original source. Distinguish historical statement, later outcome (if independently verified), and your assessment. Preserve correction uncertainties. Do not turn a source recap into unsolicited current advice.
